November 22, 2026 · A letter for your 19th

Dear Iya,

Happy 19th birthday, Iya! I hope that as you grow older, you never find yourself apologizing for being human. For feeling too much, overthinking the little things, or having days when everything just feels a little heavier than usual. I hope you never feel like you have to downplay what you're going through just because someone else might have it worse. Your feelings don't need to be measured against anyone else's struggles to be worth acknowledging. I hope you never think twice about ranting to me, no matter how big or small the things bothering you may seem. Kahit paulit-ulit pa 'yan, kahit feeling mo mababaw lang, or kahit hindi mo rin maintindihan kung bakit ka nalulungkot in the first place. You never have to make sense of everything before telling me about it. You don't have to rehearse your thoughts, find the right words, or wait until things become unbearable before you let them out. Sometimes, you just need someone to listen, and I hope you never feel guilty for needing that. I would much rather hear about the things weighing on your mind than have you convince yourself that you have to figure everything out alone. I hope you also learn to be as patient with yourself as you are with the people you love. There will be days when you'll make decisions you aren't entirely sure of, when you'll feel lost, or when you'll look at everyone else and wonder if you're falling behind. But please remember that life isn't a race, and there's no one timeline you have to follow. You are allowed to take your time. You're allowed to change your mind, make mistakes, start over, and become someone you never expected to be. You don't have to have everything figured out at 19, or even at 29. There's no deadline for becoming yourself. As you enter another year of your life, I hope you never lose the little things that make you who you are. I hope you keep finding things that excite you, people who make you feel understood, and places where you can be completely yourself. I hope you experience the kind of happiness that makes even ordinary days feel special. And when things don't go as planned, I hope you remember that one difficult chapter doesn't mean the rest of your story will be any less beautiful. And as your ate, I hope you know that you don't always have to come to me with good news or exciting stories. You can tell me about the things that upset you, the things you're embarrassed about, the things you're afraid to admit, or even the most random things that happened during your day. You don't have to worry about being too much, too emotional, or too repetitive. I may not always have the right advice, but I'll always be willing to listen. Maligayang kaarawan, Iya! Sana hindi mo madaliin ang sarili mo sa mga bagay na darating. Marami ka pang mararanasan at matututunan, kaya sana bigyan mo rin ang sarili mo ng pagkakataong magkamali at magsimula ulit. Andito lang ang ate mo, palaging handang makinig sa lahat ng kuwento mo, kahit gaano pa kaliit o kalaki. I hope you always remember that you have someone in your corner, through the good days and the not-so-good ones. Mahal ka ni Ate. Palagi.
Your one and only Ate ♡
